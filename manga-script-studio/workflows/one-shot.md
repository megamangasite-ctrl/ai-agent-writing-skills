# From a seed to the last page

**Start with** a seed or draft, audience, length, display and any fixed ending. If a named award is part of the brief, run the [entry check](../skills/manga-contest-readiness/SKILL.md) early enough to shape the work. If the creator asks only for an outline, stop after the page map.

| Pass | Action | Handoff or decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish the promise | Use [concept and hook](../skills/manga-concept-hook/SKILL.md) to write a retellable situation and draw the first image in words. Check [genre](../skills/manga-genre-adaptation/SKILL.md) or [context](../skills/manga-originality-context/SKILL.md) where the promise depends on either. | Keep the idea with a specific, page-sized proof scene. Record it in the [brief](../templates/brief-and-hook.md). |
| Find the choice | Put the lead and counterpart under [character pressure](../skills/manga-character-psychology/SKILL.md). Give each a defensible claim with [thematic depth](../skills/manga-thematic-depth/SKILL.md). | Hand off an available alternative, its real benefit, and the gain or loss of refusing it. |
| Budget the turn | Use [arc planning](../skills/manga-arc-planning/SKILL.md) to build the attempts and [pacing](../skills/manga-page-turns/SKILL.md) to map visibility. Reserve the return of a plant and a reaction beat with [emotional payoff](../skills/manga-emotional-payoff/SKILL.md). | Every story page or scroll beat has a job. An artist can identify the first visible answer to the setup. |
| Script and read | Draft with [visual writing](../skills/manga-visual-writing/SKILL.md). Use [dialogue](../skills/manga-dialogue-subtext/SKILL.md) where speech changes what the listener can do. Read the numbered script with editorial annotations covered. | File or return the complete script. Fix the most damaging causal, visibility or emotional break within the brief. |

**Stop when** the requested length is met, the central choice and its consequence are on the page, and the artist can distinguish visible content from instructions. If the brief includes a public preview, draft a separate cut using [shareability](../skills/manga-shareability/SKILL.md).

**For a page squeeze** first cut a repeated attempt and transfer its essential clue to another action. Recheck both the facing pairs and the ending's evidence after the cut.
