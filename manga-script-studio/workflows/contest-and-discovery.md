# Prepare a preview, an award packet, or both

**Start with** the available script/pages, intended audience or contest edition, actual authorship and AI use, publication history and the creator's desired destination. Run only the branch the request needs.

## When a contest matters

1. Use [contest readiness](../skills/manga-contest-readiness/SKILL.md) and the [dossier](../templates/contest-dossier.md) to capture the edition, official conditions, manuscript type, AI scope and any limits on prior exposure. Check what the creator can document. If AI drafting is disallowed for the target, respect that boundary when choosing further work.
2. Inventory the actual materials. Prepare the requested files or text that can be prepared accurately. When the rules require one, write a private jury synopsis through the outcome and a factual contribution record. Preserve the distinction between a script and an illustrated, lettered manuscript.
3. Apply the [readiness rubric](../rubrics/submission-readiness.md). Give the creator an evidence-linked status, missing items and next action. The [artistic rubric](../rubrics/artistic-craft.md) can support a separate craft read even when an edition is closed or eligibility remains unknown.

## When discovery matters

1. Use [shareability](../skills/manga-shareability/SKILL.md) to locate a spoiler boundary in the source and a particular person who might share the excerpt with someone else. Choose a crop or sequence that pays off its own premise at reading size.
2. Draft the private preview with the requested caption, alt text and credit. For a discovery campaign or comparison, add a variant and record the changed variable and a decision rule in the [discovery sheet](../templates/discovery-test.md). Use the [discovery rubric](../rubrics/reader-discovery.md) to check the cut against the story.
3. When both branches apply, keep the spoiler-full jury text and spoiler-limited public copy in plainly labeled sections. If first-publication or preview rights are uncertain, mark promotional materials **private draft, release on hold**. Preserve the entry path while producing usable private copy.

**Finish with** a reviewable packet or preview for the creator. An unscheduled private test plan is a complete discovery deliverable when distribution has not been requested. Report results only after observations actually exist.
