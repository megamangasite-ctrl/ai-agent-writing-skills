# Originality and cultural nuance

Work from the creator's named influences, setting and actual pages. Separate the reading pleasure they admire from the recognizable expression that supplies it. Also separate invented local practices from claims about real people.

1. Make a short inventory of conspicuous resemblances in premise rules, relationship, character silhouette, signature images, scene order and resolution. A shared genre device can stay. When several distinctive elements travel together, redesign the causal mechanism or distribution of power, then rewrite the scene it changes.
2. Ground the new version in a specific task, material constraint, relationship or observed place. Show how a person familiar with that world would handle something differently from a visitor. Replacing names or clothing leaves the same plot intact.
3. For a real community, narrow the claim by place, period, language, role and point of view. Where a visitor currently makes every consequential choice, look for a decision a local character has both reason and power to make. Distinguish individual practice from community-wide custom. Where the premise relies on an unfamiliar ceremony, job, honorific or institution, record a firsthand or authoritative source and what it establishes.
4. Mark each consequential detail as grounded, invented or to verify. If support is missing, use an honestly fictional alternative, write around the claim, or suggest one precise question for a knowledgeable reader. Never invent a consultation, translation, lived identity or a blanket clearance of originality.

**Deliver** a concise before/after pair showing how the new detail changes a decision, plus unresolved questions with affected page IDs. Use the [originality and context record](../../templates/originality-and-context.md) if research will continue. For entry packets, hand verified credits, sources and open permissions to the [contest specialist](../manga-contest-readiness/SKILL.md).
