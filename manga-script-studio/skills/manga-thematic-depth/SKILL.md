# Thematic depth

Phrase the theme as a question two people in this particular story could answer differently. Give each answer a real beneficiary. A person who values keeping a secret may be protecting someone who asked for privacy.

1. Locate a scene where the lead's starting belief produces a good result. Locate a second where the same belief takes something away. Let the competing view succeed at something too. If one view amounts to cruelty alone, give the conflict a more credible opponent.
2. Pick a recurring material detail with a job in the world, such as a team photograph with the substitutes outside the crop. Place it first as an ordinary object and later in different hands or in a different frame. Record who gains the power to use or change it.
3. Give the climax one decision where both values cannot be kept intact. State the gain and the residue. A victory can leave someone grieving a real loss. A joke can carry the argument through who gets the last move.
4. Remove any speech that explains the moral and reread the ending. If the question disappears, alter a witnessed action or consequence. If an explicit line belongs to the character, put pressure on it with what the image shows.

**Deliver** the question, the strongest case on each side, and three located image or action beats. Name one plausible alternate reading supported by the work and the page detail that keeps it alive.
