# Contest readiness

Identify the organizer, edition, category, official rules and the artifact actually available. Establish who created the concept, plot, script, layouts, art, lettering and translation and which steps used AI. Distinguish the rules for each stage when the organizer does.

1. Locate the edition's official rules, amendments, application form and deadline with time zone. Use the creator's supplied official text when offline. Record links or document titles, sections and date checked. When the edition, time zone or an interpretation remains uncertain, label the exact open question. Prior editions are research leads.
2. Build the [contest dossier](../../templates/contest-dossier.md). Check eligibility and team restrictions, originality and permissions, prior publication including previews, AI scope and disclosure, content and language, manuscript versus finished art, count and dimensions, credits or anonymity, rights terms, deadline and required files. Inspect actual files where possible.
3. Assign Pass, Fail, Unknown or N/A with a reason to each applicable requirement. Treat an explicit prohibition that conflicts with documented use as Fail. Silence on a material use stays Unknown until clarified. If only a script exists and the award requires drawn pages, mark the missing art as a failure of the current packet. Preserve real authorship in every declaration.
4. Apply the [readiness rules](../../rubrics/submission-readiness.md). Separate missing fixable materials from disqualifying conditions. Summarize restrictive rights terms in the organizer's own scope and flag decisions for the creator. For a jury-fit assessment, map published judging criteria and any stated weights to specific panels. Label your own craft criteria as editorial if the jury's are unpublished.
5. Draft only the requested packet components, within the edition's word and file limits. A jury synopsis should disclose the resolution when the guidelines call for it. A creator statement needs the creator's own experience and actual contribution. Leave any unsupported personal detail for them to supply. Stop at a reviewable packet unless a separate submission is explicitly requested.

### Worked reading of a rule

The 20th Japan International MANGA Award distinguished primary AI generation of the work, including plot, from certain supplemental uses and asked entrants to declare AI use. Record plot and art separately when auditing that edition. For a different edition, reopen its own rules. [20th edition, Conditions of Entry §3(4)](https://www.manga-award.mofa.go.jp/en/application/20/index.html)

SILENT MANGA AUDITION distinguishes dialogue from sound effects in its drawing guidance. That distinction can change what counts as a finished page in its format. Check the target round and its other restrictions before adapting a script. [Official rules and guidelines, §§1 and 3](https://www.manga-audition.com/rules-guidelines/)

**Deliver** the status, its strongest piece of evidence, the remaining blocker or fix, and the packet inventory. With no named target, deliver a reusable dossier and the status “Unknown — target needed.”
