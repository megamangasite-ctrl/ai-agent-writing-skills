# Artistic craft · anchored editorial read

Read the script once without the brief. Score only axes relevant to the assignment. For each, attach a page/panel, what is on the page, and a next decision. Use **U** when missing material prevents judgment. Tag script judgments as provisional where finished art or lettering determines the result.

| Axis | 0 · missing or broken | 1 · suggested | 2 · working | 3 · distinctive and integrated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specificity and voice | Situation could belong to any cast | Fresh detail decorates a familiar mechanism | A particular detail affects a decision | The story would resolve differently without this detail or viewpoint |
| Character agency | Outcome arrives independently of the lead | Choice is declared, one option has no appeal | Two credible actions, visible decision and cost | Another person can answer, refuse or transform that costly choice |
| Thematic complexity | Moral arrives apart from the action | Opposing belief is a straw person | Both values work somewhere and collide in a scene | The ending changes how an earlier act reads and preserves a genuine tension |
| Visual storytelling | Essential change exists only in notes | Images illustrate explanatory speech | Blocking, action or object carries essential information | Framing, marks, repetition or negative space reveal meaning prose would lose |
| Rhythm and release | Order or visibility breaks the intended inference | Timing is declared but a nearby beat competes | The display conceals or displays the answer as intended on the page map | Layout also gives anticipation or aftermath a specific felt duration |
| Voice and silence | Speaker or intention is unreadable | Speech repeats the image or voices blur | Speech, SFX or purposeful silence express distinct tactics | Image and language complicate each other, or sustained silence carries an equally precise exchange |
| Earned emotional payoff | Ending has no available cause or plant | A twist or speech announces a feeling | An earlier detail returns through a consequential action | The last image rewards rereading while allowing a specific feeling to remain unresolved |

**Use the scores.** Identify one protected strength, the bottleneck that most changes the intended experience, and a revision whose effect can be tested. Keep the axes as a profile. Averages can conceal a broken reveal or an unearned ending.

**Calibration.** An offered hand and a return to last year's photograph can give agency and payoff strong script evidence. Final balloon legibility still needs actual lettering. A disagreement about whether a final smile is necessary belongs in the note with evidence for both readings.
