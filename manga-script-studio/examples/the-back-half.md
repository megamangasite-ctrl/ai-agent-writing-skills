# The Back Half

*Original teaching script · six story pages · black and white · right-to-left print*

**Artist and letterer.** P1 is a single left page. P2 right faces P3 left, P4 right faces P5 left, and P6 right faces a blank endpaper. Nell and Bo are teens in a small theatre company. Nell wears striped socks, Bo has a darned elbow. Their stage horse has a cardboard head, a bent ear, a fabric body and a light rear hoop. The fabric fastens to the head and hoop and hangs loose along the sides. The horse faces left as it enters. The photographer works from the aisle. Numbered panels give reading order. Only indented lettered lines appear as text in the art.

## Page 1 · left · 3 panels

**P1.1** — In the wing, three instant photos hang from clips above a makeup mirror. In each, a different performer beams from beneath the lifted head of the same handsome horse. Two striped socks stick out beneath its rump. A fourth clip hangs empty. Bo's finger enters the frame at the newest print.

  T1 · LABELS ABOVE THE THREE CLIPS — 2023  /  2024  /  2025

  D2 · BO, OFF — Which one's you?

**P1.2** — Bo holds the newest print beside Nell's ankle. The wide and narrow stripes on the real sock match the ones under the horse's tail.

  D1 · NELL, OFF — The socks.

**P1.3** — Nell balances the horse's head on her own. In the mirror her grin sits just beneath its square teeth. She pinches its crooked cardboard ear straight.

  D1 · NELL — Stay.

## Page 2 · right · 4 panels

**P2.1** — From the end of the aisle, the photographer raises the instant camera toward the gap in the curtain. In the gap the horse waits on four legs, its bent ear just clear of the drape.

  D1 · PHOTOGRAPHER — One shot left, Nell. Head up after the bow.

**P2.2** — Under the fabric. Bo's cheek rests against the rear hoop. Ahead of her she can see the backs of Nell's knees.

  D1 · BO — Wave if you see an oven mitt.

**P2.3** — A sliver of Nell's eye in the horse's open mouth. Leave air above and behind the muzzle for Bo's reply.

  D1 · NELL — An oven mitt?

  D2 · BO, OFF — Mum says a hand's too small.

**P2.4** — Low along the stage boards. The striped socks are through the curtain, and the other pair of shoes is just crossing the bright line. The hem cuts a single dark shape across all four legs.

## Page 3 · left · 4 panels

**P3.1** — In profile the horse makes a very good bow. Nose low, back high, all four shoes planted. From here it looks like one animal.

**P3.2** — The pose from the photographs. Nell has lifted the head to her shoulder. Her chin is up for the camera. Behind her, Bo is still a round, convincing rump.

**P3.3** — From Nell's height, a pale quilted oven mitt rises above the bare hands in the audience. It has one scorched thumb.

**P3.4** — At the loose side seam of the horse, Nell reaches back into the dark. A strip of stage light catches her empty palm.

## Page 4 · right · 1 panel

**P4.1** — Wide, with room above both heads. Bo stands upright, one hand in Nell's and the other holding the rear hoop at shoulder height. The cardboard head rests against Nell's opposite shoulder. Between the two girls the empty belly sags nearly to the floor. Its rough patches and enormous stitches face the audience. The animal has never looked less like a horse. Both faces are in the light.

## Page 5 · left · 3 panels

**P5.1** — Bo's waving hand, bare, above the hoop. Across the dark aisle the oven mitt is tipped toward it at exactly the same angle. Give the pair a little pocket of quiet on the page.

**P5.2** — The bad ear has folded over Nell's eyebrow. Her fingers are halfway up to straighten it. Bo's eyes are on Nell.

  D1 · BO — Leave it.

**P5.3** — A small panel. In the white flash, the slumped cloth, the bent ear and two heads turned toward each other make a ragged silhouette.

## Page 6 · right · 3 panels

**P6.1** — Back in the wing. The fourth clip holds a print now. In it, the horse's ruined silhouette droops between the two performers. For the first time in the row, nobody is looking at the camera.

**P6.2** — The oldest photo lies flat on the makeup bench. Nell holds its bottom corners still. Bo's uncapped pen hovers over the white margin.

**P6.3** — Close on the white margin of that old print, still between Nell's fingers. In Bo's loose block letters, a name and a small arrow. The point of the arrow just touches a striped sock under the rump.

  T1 · PHOTO MARGIN — NELL
