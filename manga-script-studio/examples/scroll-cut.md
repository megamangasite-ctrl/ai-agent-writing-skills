# The Back Half · the offer on a phone

*Worked adaptation of P3.2–P5.1 for continuous vertical scrolling. The earlier bow and the later flash remain part of the full story. This passage has no lettering.*

**Retain** Nell's chance at the traditional portrait, the sight of the mitt, the private offer, Bo's answer and the returned wave. In print, a sheet of paper hides the answer. On a phone, the lower edge of one reader's screen may already show what the next reader has yet to uncover.

**Why B4 is new.** The print version lets a page turn elide Bo taking the hand. The scroll version gives the answer in two pieces. Even if the reader glimpses B4 while holding B3, the grip creates anticipation for what the pair will do onstage.
