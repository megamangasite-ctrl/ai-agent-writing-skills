### Filled miniature · The Third Bell

A bicycle courier repairs dead doorbells on deliveries. A working bell grants its tenant a choice about opening the door. New tenants and different failures sustain the cases. Tenants who answer become recurring neighbors.

| Chapter | Local settlement | Continuing obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · planned | A sleeping baker misses deliveries. The courier fits a bell that tugs a ribbon over the kneading bench. The baker signs for the flour. | The bakery now opens before the courier's shift. The baker gives her a spare key and asks her to wake the upstairs tenant. |
| 2 · planned | The upstairs tenant is awake and deliberately disconnected the buzzer. The courier agrees to use an agreed window signal for that tenant's parcels. | She returns the key. The baker must ask his neighbor directly about sharing the entrance. |
| 3 · planned | A jammed entrance traps both tenants' parcels outside. They design a two-name delivery board together. | This address can now receive a parcel when either tenant is home. Later routes inherit that change. |
