# Artist-facing script page

Copy the block for each page. Include the title, sequence length, reading direction, recurring cast identifiers and essential prop geography once. The sample notation numbers panels in reading order. D denotes dialogue, C a caption, S a sound effect and T readable in-world text. The number gives the order across **all** lettered items in that panel.

```text
[TITLE] • [PAGE TOTAL OR SCROLL LENGTH] • [DISPLAY / DIRECTION]
Setup — [fixed geography, recurring silhouettes, relevant continuity only]

PAGE [n] • [left/right/single] • [panel count] panels
[Optional private production note about the page's dominant beat]

P[n].1 — [One drawable instant. Name the focal action and required relationship.]
  D1 · [SPEAKER, OFF if needed] — [exact lettered words]
  S2 · [placement or source if essential] — [exact visible sound]

P[n].2 — [A distinct instant, with space for the speaker's balloon.]
  C1 · [narrator/time if relevant] — [exact caption]
  T2 · [sign/prop] — [exact in-world text]

P[n].3 — [Reaction or changed state. Omit lettering when silence is intended.]
```

Use only the labels the panel needs. Mark a *fully wordless* scene explicitly when all balloons, readable signs, captions and written SFX are excluded. For scroll, replace page/panel IDs with stable beat IDs such as B1–B6 and write where the reader encounters the next piece of information. Specify a spread or framing constraint only where it carries a story requirement.

**Filled panel**

> P3.4 — At the loose side seam of the horse, Nell reaches back into the dark. A strip of stage light catches her empty palm.

The reader can see the offer. The next visible unit decides whether Bo accepts it. The sentence explaining that inference belongs in editorial notes, outside the script.
